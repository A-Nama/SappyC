## What is SappyC?

Tired of seeing the same generic *"Congrats on the new role!"*, *"So inspiring! 🙌"*, and *"Agree? Thoughts?"* corporate jargon clogging up your feed?

SappyC is here to restore balance to the universe. Paste any pretentious LinkedIn post into our bright pink aesthetic app, and SappyC will generate ultra-sassy, sappy, and delightfully unhinged response options designed to disrupt the hustle culture matrix.

---

## Features

-  **Pink & Cutesy Aesthetic** — Pastel-powered corporate destruction.
-  **AI-Powered Sarcasm** — Custom comment generation crafts the ultimate response options.
-  **Meme Roulette** — Pair your selected comment with a randomly selected meme for maximum emotional damage.
-  **Instant Web App** — No setup needed, start causing chaos right from your browser!

## How to Use

1. Head over to [sappyc.streamlit.app](https://sappyc.streamlit.app/).
2. Hit **"START THE CHAOS"**.
3. Paste any generic or humble-brag LinkedIn post into the text area.
4. Click **"Generate Comments"**.
5. Pick your favorite sassy comment.
6. Receive your paired meme, copy the text, and **POST IT IF YOU DARE!** 😜🔥
